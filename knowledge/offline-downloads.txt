# Offline Downloads

Offline downloads let you save music and podcasts to a device so you can listen with no internet connection, for example on a plane or underground. Downloading music is available on every Premium plan (Individual, Duo, Family, and Student). Resonance Free listeners cannot download music, but they can download podcast episodes.

How to download: open an album, playlist, or podcast and tap the downward arrow icon. A green arrow means the item is fully downloaded. You can download whole albums, playlists, and podcasts, but not single songs on their own; to keep one song offline, add it to a playlist and download that playlist. Your Liked Songs collection can also be downloaded as a whole.

Limits: each account can download up to 10,000 tracks on each device, on up to 5 devices at the same time. If you try to download on a sixth device, Resonance asks you to remove one of the others first. To free up a device slot, go to Account, then Devices, then Offline devices, and choose Remove next to the device you no longer use. Removing a device deletes its downloads the next time it goes online.

Stay online once a month: downloaded music stays playable only if the device connects to the internet at least once every 30 days. This lets Resonance confirm your subscription and pay artists for offline plays. If a device has been offline for more than 30 days, its downloads are locked until it reconnects; nothing is deleted, the downloads unlock automatically as soon as the device is back online.

Download quality: in Settings, then Audio quality, then Download, you can choose Normal (96 kbps), High (160 kbps), Very high (320 kbps), or Lossless (FLAC, up to 24-bit/96 kHz). The default is High. Lossless files are large: an average album takes about 300 MB in Lossless compared with about 70 MB in High. Changing the quality only affects new downloads; to convert existing ones, remove and download them again.

Mobile data: by default Resonance downloads only over Wi-Fi. To allow downloads over mobile data, turn on Settings, then Audio quality, then Download using cellular. Streaming over mobile data is controlled by a separate setting.

Storage location: on Android you can store downloads on an SD card in Settings, then Storage, then Download location. On iPhone and iPad downloads always use internal storage. To see how much space downloads use, or to delete all of them at once, open Settings, then Storage, then Remove all downloads.

Offline mode: turn on Settings, then Playback, then Offline mode to play only what is downloaded, even when you have a connection. This is useful to avoid using mobile data by accident.

If you cancel Premium or your payment fails and the account moves to Resonance Free, all downloaded music is removed from your devices at the end of the billing period. Downloaded podcast episodes are kept.
