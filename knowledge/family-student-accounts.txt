# Family and Student Accounts

The Folio Family plan covers up to 6 accounts, including the plan manager, who all live at the same home address. Each member uses their own login and has a private library, so members cannot see what the others are reading unless they choose to share a book.

Inviting members: the plan manager goes to Account, then Family, then Invite member, and sends an invite link by email or text. The link expires after 7 days. The person accepting confirms the home address when they join. A person can belong to only one Family plan at a time, and can switch to a different Family plan only once every 12 months.

Sharing books: books that a family member buys can be shared with the whole plan. Open the book, tap the three dots, then choose Share with family. Shared books appear in every member's library under Family shelf. The plan manager can stop sharing any book at any time. Unlimited books are not shared; each member borrows their own copy, and each member has their own limit of 20 borrowed books.

Audiobook credits on Family: the plan includes 2 audiobook credits per month. Credits go into a shared family pool that the plan manager controls. The manager can let every member spend credits freely, or require approval before a member uses one.

Kids profiles: the plan manager can turn any member account into a kids profile for readers under 13. Kids profiles show only books rated for children, hide the store, and cannot buy books or spend credits. To set one up, go to Account, then Family, choose the member, then turn on Kids profile. You can also set a daily reading time goal and see reading progress reports.

Removing members: the plan manager can remove a member in Account, then Family. The removed member moves to Folio Free and keeps the books they bought, but loses access to shared books and borrowed Unlimited books.

Folio Student: the Student plan is for students aged 18 or older at an accredited college or university. Folio checks enrollment through a verification partner when you sign up. You must reverify every 12 months, and you can stay on Folio Student for at most 48 months in total. If verification fails or the 48 months run out, your plan changes to Folio Unlimited at the regular price at your next billing date, and Folio emails you 7 days before.

Students cannot be members of a Family plan and on Folio Student at the same time.
