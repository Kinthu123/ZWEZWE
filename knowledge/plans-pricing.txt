# Plans and Pricing

Resonance offers one free plan and four Premium plans. All prices are in US dollars, billed monthly unless stated otherwise, and local sales tax may be added at checkout. Prices on this page took effect on 1 March 2026.

Resonance Free costs nothing and is supported by ads. On phones and tablets, Free listeners can play albums and playlists only in shuffle mode and can skip up to 6 tracks per hour. Free streams at up to 160 kbps, cannot download music for offline listening, and plays an audio or video ad roughly every 30 minutes of listening. On desktop and the web player, Free listeners can pick any track on demand.

Premium Individual costs $10.99 per month for one account. It removes all ads, allows unlimited skips, lets you play any track in any order on every device, unlocks offline downloads, and includes lossless audio (Resonance Hi-Fi, up to 24-bit/96 kHz FLAC) at no extra cost. Premium Individual can also be paid yearly at $109.90 per year, which works out to two months free compared with paying monthly.

Premium Duo costs $14.99 per month for two Premium accounts that live at the same address. Each person keeps their own library, playlists, and recommendations, and Duo members also get a shared playlist called Duo Mix that updates every Wednesday.

Premium Family costs $17.99 per month for up to 6 Premium accounts that live at the same address, including the plan manager. Family includes Resonance Kids, a separate app for children under 13 with explicit content blocked. Details on inviting members and the address rules are in the Family and Student Accounts guide.

Premium Student costs $5.99 per month for one account and has the same features as Premium Individual. It is available to students aged 18 or over enrolled at an accredited college or university, and requires verification. Eligibility rules are in the Family and Student Accounts guide.

Free trial: people who have never had Premium, Duo, Family, or Student before can try Premium Individual free for 1 month. Duo and Family trials are also 1 month, and Student trials are 1 month. A payment method is required to start a trial, you are charged automatically when the trial ends unless you cancel at least 24 hours before, and each person can only have one Premium trial ever, across all plans.

Changing plans: you can switch between Premium plans at any time from Account, then Subscription, then Change plan. Upgrading (for example from Individual to Family) takes effect immediately and you are charged a prorated amount for the rest of the current billing period. Downgrading takes effect at the start of your next billing period.

Gift cards: Resonance gift cards come in values of $10.99, $32.97 (3 months), $65.94 (6 months), and $109.90 (12 months). They can only be redeemed for Premium Individual, cannot be used for Duo, Family, or Student, and never expire.

Payment methods accepted: Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, PayPal, Resonance gift cards, and Apple or Google in app purchase. Subscriptions bought through the Apple App Store or Google Play are billed and managed by those stores and cost $2 more per month.
