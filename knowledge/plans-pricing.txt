# Plans and Pricing

Folio is a reading service for ebooks and audiobooks. You can read and listen in the Folio app on your phone, tablet, computer, or e-reader. Folio has four plans: Folio Free, Folio Unlimited, Folio Family, and Folio Student. Prices on this page are in US dollars and include sales tax where it applies.

Folio Free costs nothing. Free readers can read free classics and public domain books, read the first chapter of any book as a sample, and buy individual ebooks and audiobooks at their listed price. Free readers cannot borrow from the Unlimited catalog and cannot download audiobooks they have not bought.

Folio Unlimited costs $11.99 per month for one person. It includes unlimited reading from the Unlimited catalog of more than 1.5 million ebooks, plus one audiobook credit every month. You can have up to 20 Unlimited books borrowed at the same time. Unlimited is also available as a yearly plan for $119.99 per year, which is about two months free compared with paying monthly.

Folio Family costs $17.99 per month for up to 6 accounts that live at the same address. Every member gets their own library, reading progress, and recommendations, and the plan includes 2 audiobook credits per month that the plan manager can share with any member. The plan manager pays for the plan and can add or remove members at any time.

Folio Student costs $5.99 per month for students at an accredited college or university. It includes everything in Folio Unlimited. Eligibility rules are described in the Family and Student Accounts help page.

Free trial: new readers can try Folio Unlimited free for 30 days. Only one free trial is allowed per person and per payment method. If you do not cancel before the trial ends, the monthly Unlimited price is charged automatically on day 31.

Changing plans: to upgrade, go to Account, then Plan, then Change plan. Upgrades start immediately and you pay only the difference for the rest of the current billing period. Downgrades start at the next billing date, so you keep your current plan until then. If you downgrade from Unlimited to Free, borrowed Unlimited books are returned at the end of the billing period, but books you bought stay in your library forever.

Payment: Folio accepts credit and debit cards, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and Folio gift cards. Gift cards come in $25, $50, and $100 values and never expire. To redeem one, go to Account, then Redeem gift card. The gift card balance is used before your card is charged.
