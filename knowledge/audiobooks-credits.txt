# Audiobooks and Credits

An audiobook credit lets you get one audiobook to keep, whatever its listed price. Folio Unlimited and Folio Student include 1 credit every month, and Folio Family includes 2 credits per month for the whole plan. Folio Free readers do not get credits but can buy audiobooks at their listed price.

Using a credit: open any audiobook in the store and tap Use 1 credit. The audiobook is added to your library and is yours to keep, even if you cancel your plan later. Credits can be used on any audiobook in the store, including new releases. Credits cannot be used on ebooks.

When credits arrive: your new credit is added on your billing date every month. Unused credits roll over, and you can keep up to 6 unused credits at a time. Once you hold 6, no new credit is added until you spend one. If you cancel your paid plan, any unused credits expire at the end of the billing period, so spend them before you cancel.

Buying extra credits: readers on a paid plan can buy a bundle of 3 extra credits for $29.99 in Account, then Credits, then Buy more. Extra credits never expire while your plan is active.

Returning an audiobook: if you do not enjoy an audiobook you got with a credit, you can return it within 365 days of getting it and receive the credit back. Go to Library, tap the three dots on the audiobook, then choose Return for credit. You can return up to 3 audiobooks in any 12 months. Audiobooks bought with money are refunded as described in the Refunds and Cancellation help page.

Audiobook previews: every audiobook has a free 5 minute sample. Tap Play sample on the book page to listen before you spend a credit.

Unlimited audiobooks: some audiobooks are marked Included in Unlimited. Readers on a paid plan can listen to those without using a credit, and they count toward the 20 borrowed book limit. They are returned automatically if your plan ends.

Narration and accessibility: each audiobook page lists the narrator, length, and whether the book is narrated by a person or with an AI voice. Chapters can be skipped using the chapter list, and you can add bookmarks with a note at any point while listening.
