# Refunds and Cancellation

You can cancel any Premium plan at any time, with no cancellation fee. This guide explains what happens when you cancel, when you can get money back, and what happens if a payment fails.

How to cancel: on the web or desktop app, go to Account, then Subscription, then Cancel Premium, and confirm. You cannot cancel from inside the iPhone or Android app unless you subscribed through the Apple App Store or Google Play, in which case you must cancel in your App Store or Google Play subscription settings instead; Resonance cannot cancel those for you.

What happens after you cancel: you keep Premium until the end of the billing period you already paid for. After that the account moves to Resonance Free. Your playlists, Liked Songs, and followed artists are kept, but downloaded music is removed from your devices and you go back to ads and shuffle play on mobile. If you are the manager of a Family or Duo plan, cancelling moves every member to Resonance Free at the same time.

Refunds on monthly plans: monthly payments are not refunded for partial months, and cancelling does not refund the current month. Resonance does refund in full when you were charged by mistake: a duplicate charge, a charge after you cancelled, or a charge for a trial you cancelled at least 24 hours before it ended. Request these within 60 days of the charge.

Refunds on the yearly plan: if you cancel Premium Individual yearly within 14 days of being charged, you get a full refund and Premium ends right away. After 14 days there is no refund for the unused months, but you keep Premium until the end of the year you paid for.

Gift cards and in app purchases: gift cards are not refundable and cannot be exchanged for cash. Refunds for subscriptions bought through the Apple App Store or Google Play are handled only by Apple or Google under their own policies.

How refunds are paid: approved refunds go back to the original payment method and normally arrive within 5 to 10 business days. Refunds to PayPal usually arrive within 3 business days. Resonance cannot refund to a different card or account.

Failed payments: if a payment fails, Resonance retries the charge 3 times over 7 days and emails you each time. Premium keeps working during those 7 days (the grace period). If all retries fail, the account moves to Resonance Free and downloads are removed. Updating your payment method in Account, then Payment, during the grace period charges the new method straight away and keeps Premium without interruption.

Restarting Premium: you can resubscribe any time from Account, then Subscription. Your playlists are still there, but downloads must be downloaded again, and you are not eligible for another free trial.

Deleting your account is different from cancelling: it permanently removes your profile, playlists, and listening history. Request it from Account, then Privacy, then Close account. You have 30 days to change your mind by signing in again; after 30 days the deletion cannot be undone. Close any paid plan first, because deleting the account does not refund the current billing period.

Contacting support about a charge: use Help, then Contact us, then Billing in the app or at help.resonance.example. Live chat is open 24 hours a day, and billing emails to billing@resonance.example are answered within 2 business days.
