# Refunds and Cancellation

You can cancel your Folio plan at any time with no cancellation fee. To cancel, go to Account, then Plan, then Cancel plan, and follow the steps. If you subscribed through the Apple App Store or Google Play, you must cancel there instead, because they handle the billing.

What happens when you cancel: your paid plan keeps working until the end of the current billing period, and you are not charged again. After that, your account moves to Folio Free. Borrowed Unlimited books are returned, unused audiobook credits expire, and downloaded Unlimited books are removed from your devices. Every ebook and audiobook you bought or got with a credit stays in your library for good, along with your highlights and notes.

Monthly plans: monthly charges are not refunded for partly used months. If you were charged for a new month by mistake and you have not borrowed a book, listened to an Unlimited audiobook, or used a credit since that charge, contact support within 7 days of the charge for a full refund of that month.

Yearly plan: if you cancel the Unlimited yearly plan within 14 days of the charge, you get a full refund. After 14 days the yearly plan is not refunded, but it keeps working until the end of the paid year.

Free trial: if you cancel during the 30 day free trial, you are never charged. If you forget to cancel and are charged on day 31, you can ask for a refund within 48 hours of that first charge, as long as you have not used the month's audiobook credit.

Ebook refunds: an ebook you bought can be returned within 7 days of purchase for a full refund, as long as you have read less than 10 percent of it. Go to Library, tap the three dots on the book, then Request refund.

Audiobook refunds: an audiobook bought with money can be refunded within 14 days of purchase if you have listened to less than 15 percent of it. Audiobooks got with a credit are returned for a credit instead, as described in the Audiobooks and Credits help page.

How refunds are paid: refunds go back to the original payment method and take 5 to 10 business days to appear. Purchases paid with a Folio gift card are refunded to your gift card balance.

Deleting your account: to close your Folio account completely, cancel any paid plan first, then go to Account, then Privacy, then Delete account. Deleting your account removes your library permanently, including books you bought, and cannot be undone.
